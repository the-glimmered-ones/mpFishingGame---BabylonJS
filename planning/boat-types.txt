can change rigging type at port from gaff to bermuda with resource cost
gaff - looks cool, faster off wind, more control and faster deccel, much more sail
bermuda - simpler design, can sail closer to windward (source) direction
wind coming from starboard side, sails on port side = starboard tack; wind from port side = port tack
changing one tack to the other while bringing the wind direction across the bow is tacking (upwind)
tacking into the wind at close reach still allows forward progress
changing tack with the wind to the stern (downwind) is called jibing, haul in sail during turn, so wind catches it on the other side
sailing at broad reach (downwind at a diagonal) is faster then running (straight downwind), so repeatedly jibing in a zig zag is faster than sailing in a straight line
